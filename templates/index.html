Supported Allergens

We check for all 9 major allergens recognized by the FDA, including synonyms, derivatives and hidden names.

🥛 Milk & Dairy 🥜 Peanuts 🌰 Tree Nuts 🥚 Eggs 🫘 Soy 🌾 Wheat / Gluten 🐟 Fish 🦐 Shellfish 🫙 Sesame
Coverage includes hidden names: casein & whey (milk), arachis oil (peanuts), albumin (eggs), tahini (sesame), surimi (fish), worcestershire sauce (fish), and many more.

Important Limitations

⚠️ OCR Accuracy

Poor lighting, blurry photos or unusual fonts can cause OCR errors. Always verify extracted text against the physical label.

⚠️ Cross-Contact

This tool cannot detect "may contain" warnings or manufacturing cross-contact. Those risks are not in ingredient lists.

🚫 Not Medical Advice

A clean scan result does not mean a food is safe. Consult your allergist or healthcare provider for medical guidance.

ℹ️ Dictionary Coverage

Our allergen dictionary is comprehensive but not exhaustive. Novel product names or brand-specific terms may be missed.
